Everything a headless machine needs from you

Imaging a new PC, rescuing one that won't boot, or clicking through yet another Windows setup: do it from your desk, in one window.

🖥️

See it and drive it

Live video of the target in a window. Click it and your keyboard and mouse belong to the target. Ctrl+Alt+Esc gives them back.

⚡

Works from power-on

BIOS and UEFI menus, OS installers, login screens, recovery shells. To the target it is just a USB keyboard and mouse, so nothing needs installing.

⌨️

The keys your PC keeps

Ctrl+Alt+Del, the Windows key, Alt+Tab: one click each. On Windows and Mac, capture sends them to the target live instead of to your own desktop.

🔁

Replay the boring parts

Script Windows setup, an installer or a BIOS tour once and replay it, with steps that wait for the right screen. Preview and dry-run first.

🌐

Boot from the network

Turn on the adapter's built-in, read-only network-boot drive with one click, and install a bare UEFI machine over the network. Off until you want it.

⏺️

Record the session

Save what happened as a GIF to share or a WebM with sound, for docs, bug reports and handovers.

Up and running in three steps

No toolchains, no drivers, no terminal. If you can double-click an installer, you can do this.

Get two parts

An ESP32-S3 board with two USB-C ports (about ten dollars) and any cheap USB HDMI capture card.

Install the app

Download it below. The board's firmware is inside the app: plug the board in and flash it with one click.

Plug in and drive

Board into the target's USB, capture card onto its HDMI. Pair once over Bluetooth, and the target is on your screen.

Secure by design, not as an afterthought

A box that can type into your machines should be hard to misuse. kvm-it is built that way.

  • Pairing needs your hands on the hardware. The adapter only accepts a new controller for a few minutes after you press its button.
  • Passwords are masked, never logged, never saved.
  • Nothing is installed on the target, and the network-boot drive is read-only: the target cannot change it.
  • Signed Windows releases, and every download ships with a checksum.
